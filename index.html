The Best Movie I Have Seen Lately

Whiplash

The movie Whiplash is about a young jazz drummer named Andrew, in a music college trying to pursue his dream of becoming one of the best drummers. He is selected to join an elite jazz band led by a strict music instructor. Throughout the movie, Andrew faces intense pressure, criticism, and emotional challenges as his jazz instructor pushes him beyond his limits to achieve perfection. Andrew becomes obsessed with improving his drumming skills. He begins sacrificing his relationships, personal life, and well-being to pursue his dream. It ends with him going overboard with his obsession and losing everything, but he manages to regain the enjoyment of playing the drums.

One of the best movies I have seen lately is Whiplash. I have been playing the drums since I was a kid, and I still play them today, so this movie really resonated with me on a personal level. Since I have years of experience playing the drums, I was able to understand many of the musical terms and techniques in the movie. What made the movie even more interesting to me was how it showed the emotional and psychological challenges of being a musician. I found the movie to be extremely stressful and invoked alot of emotion. At one point I found myself covering my eyes with my hands. The intense drumming performances were especially exciting to watch because I could recognize how technically difficult some of the pieces were.

The movie Whiplash by Damien Chazelle.
